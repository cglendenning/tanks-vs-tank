# The questions that never change

The business has one goal: lifetime gross profit to CAC (LTGP:CAC), at least $3 earned for every $1 spent to
bring in a player. These questions stay the same forever; what changes is the constraint, the one thing that most
holds the goal back today. Every answer is ONE action that can be taken today.

## The question

**What is the ONE thing I can do today to raise lifetime gross profit to CAC?**

It is answered through whichever of these the current constraint sits under:

1. **What is the ONE thing I can do today to get more organic installs from players who buy?**
   Free players cost nothing, so every one who buys raises LTGP:CAC directly. Levers: store pages (screenshots,
   preview, custom product pages, keywords), organic clips and posts, ratings and reviews, word of mouth from inside
   the game.
2. **What is the ONE thing I can do today to lower what I pay for each paid player who buys?**
   Levers: the channel mix and each channel's budget and bid goal, the ad hooks (taps per ad shown), the store page
   the ad lands on (installs per page view), turning off what costs too much.
3. **What is the ONE thing I can do today to point ads at players who buy?**
   Levers: the event each channel optimizes for (installs, then purchases once there are enough), countries and
   devices, audiences, hooks that attract spenders rather than browsers.
4. **What is the ONE thing I can do today to make the offer so good players feel stupid saying no?**
   Levers: the value equation (dream outcome, how likely and how fast it feels, how little effort and sacrifice),
   what each pack contains and costs, when and where an offer appears, and keeping players long enough to see it
   (day 1, 7 and 30).

## How to answer

1. **Find the constraint.** Use the Shred HQ Constraint screen when Craig shares it; otherwise pull the figures
   directly (AppLovin's report, App Store Connect's sales and analytics reports, Google Play's reports bucket) and
   apply the same rule: a source not updated, then a channel too thin to read, then the figure furthest below its
   target.
2. **Pick the question** the constraint sits under, then the lever with the biggest expected effect on LTGP:CAC for
   the least of Craig's time.
3. **Give exactly ONE action**, written as:
   - **Today's ONE action:** an imperative Craig can do now (or that Claude does now and reports).
   - **Why:** the constraint and the figure behind it.
   - **Expected effect:** which figure moves, which way, and when it will be read.
4. **Never answer "wait for data to bake."** If the data is thin, the action is one that makes data or volume now:
   fix what stops a channel serving, post a clip, change a hook, adjust a budget within the experiment, ship the
   build that is ready, or decide something that is waiting on a decision.
5. **Prefer what Claude can do alone.** Ask Craig only for what needs him (a console with no API, a purchase, a
   decision), as one action with its exact clicks.
