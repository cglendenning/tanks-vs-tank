# Tread Shred, Hook E v3: "Bless Your Heart" (about 15 s, portrait 9:16)

The first version's story, characters and room (Craig's favourite), played as broad redneck comedy: loud, specific,
affectionate. She is crazy and funny, never just mean. Dale is the deadpan straight man.

## Cast (describe them exactly like this in every shot)
- BRANDI: mid 20s, cute in a trashy way. Round freckled face, small gap in her front teeth, bleach-blonde hair with
  grown-out dark roots in a messy high ponytail with a claw clip, big gold hoop earrings, thick winged eyeliner, long
  hot-pink acrylic nails, oversized leopard-print hoodie, chewing gum with her mouth open. Voice: loud, nasal, thick
  rural Southern twang (Appalachian, "ain't", "dang", dropped g's). Big theatrical faces.
- DALE: late 20s, scruffy beard, backwards camo trucker cap, gray tank top. Back to the camera the whole ad. Voice: slow,
  low, flat country drawl. Never raises his voice.
- ROOM: night, cluttered single-wide trailer living room: wood paneling, string lights, a box fan, a worn plaid couch,
  crushed beer cans and an overflowing ashtray on the coffee table.
- THE PHONE: always held sideways like a game controller. Its screen is real Tread Shred gameplay (top-down tank
  combat on a blue tiled arena). When generating video, render the screen solid chroma-key green; the real gameplay is
  keyed in afterwards, so no invented game footage ever appears.

## Music (added in the edit, never generated inside the shots)
- 0 s to the grab: a plucky, comedic bluegrass banjo, low under the dialogue.
- The moment she has the phone: the banjo stops dead and Tread Shred's own in-game tank music slams in, carrying her
  "you hid this from me" and the end card.
