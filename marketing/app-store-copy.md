# Tread Shred — App Store copy

## Promotional text

Lock on. Fire smart. Shred every mission.

## Description

Tread Shred is precision tank combat built for fast, brutal missions. Drive the line, read the ricochet, and destroy every hostile before the arena closes in.

FEATURES
• 40 tactical missions
• Ricochet shots and deliberate positioning
• Heavy armor, aggressive enemies, and live-fire arenas
• Campaign score that carries across the operation
• Rewarded Overdrive: watch a brief ad to arm double rounds for your next mission
• Free to play

OVERDRIVE
Need more firepower? Trigger Overdrive after a mission and deploy with double rounds on the next screen. Two barrels. One brutal advantage. The boost lasts for one mission, and you can re-arm it after every screen.

HOW TO PLAY
Use the joystick to maneuver. Tap the arena to fire. Use walls, timing, and angles to outmaneuver the enemy. Only the strongest crews finish the operation.
