## Description

Lock on. Fire. Shred everything in your path.

Tread Shred is fast, brutal tank combat. Drive into the arena, bank your shots off the walls and destroy every enemy tank before they destroy you. Every kill hits hard: the screen shakes, the wreck explodes and the battlefield knows you were there.

BUILD A WAR MACHINE
Every mission pays coins. Spend them on permanent upgrades that never go away:
• ARMOR: take up to 8 hits instead of 3
• FIREPOWER: up to double damage per shot
• SPEED: up to 50% faster
Ten levels per upgrade. Your tank gets stronger every time you play.

40 MISSIONS. THEN IT GETS HARDER.
Clear all 40 missions to unlock VETERAN: tougher, faster enemies and double the coins. Beat Veteran to unlock ELITE, where enemies hit back harder still and every mission pays triple.

NEVER GO DOWN WITHOUT A FIGHT
Tank destroyed? Revive on the spot and finish the job. Lost a mission? Redeploy with an extra hit of armor. Won it? Arm Overdrive and fire two rounds per tap on the next one.

NO ADS. EVER.
No pop-ups, no videos, no interruptions. Just combat.

HOW TO PLAY
Steer with the joystick. Tap to fire. Use the walls, your angles and your timing to outgun every enemy.

Tread Shred is free to play. Gems are an optional in-app purchase used for revives, boosts and faster upgrades. Every mission can be played and won without buying anything.
