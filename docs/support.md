# Tread Shred Support

Need help with Tread Shred? Email **c_glendenning@yahoo.com**. Tell us whether you play on iPhone, iPad or Android,
your device model, and what happened.

## Purchases

- Gems arrive as soon as the App Store or Google Play confirms the purchase. If a purchase does not arrive, open the
  game again while online: it completes any purchase the store has confirmed. If the gems still have not arrived,
  email us with your store receipt.
- Refunds are handled by the store: on iPhone and iPad at https://reportaproblem.apple.com, and on Android from your
  Google Play order history at https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.

## Saved progress

Progress is saved to iCloud on iPhone and iPad and to Google Play Games on Android. After a reinstall or on a new
device, sign in with the same Apple account or Google account and your progress comes back.
