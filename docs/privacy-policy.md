# Tread Shred Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026. This policy covers Tread Shred on iPhone, iPad and Android, published by TGTStudios
(Craig Glendenning), "we" below.

## The short version

- Tread Shred has no ads, no accounts and no sign-up.
- We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- On iPhone and iPad, Tread Shred does not track you: it never reads the advertising identifier and never asks for
  tracking permission.

## What the game collects

### Gameplay analytics (iPhone, iPad and Android)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn how the game is played so we can improve it. It collects:

- an identifier created for this installation of the game (not your advertising identifier);
- gameplay events, such as missions started, won and lost, upgrades bought and purchases made;
- your device model, operating system and the game's version;
- your approximate location (country or region), worked out from your IP address.

This data is not linked to your identity.

### Purchases (iPhone, iPad and Android)

Gem packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle your payment; we never see your payment
details. We receive a record of each purchase (the product, its price and the transaction) so the game can deliver
your gems. Unity In-App Purchasing, made by Unity Technologies, connects the game to the store and may report purchase
events to Unity together with an identifier for this installation.

### Ad campaign measurement (Android only)

When you install Tread Shred from an ad, we want to know which ad brought you. On Android the game uses Tenjin, which
reads your device's advertising ID and app set ID and records when you open the game and the purchases the store
confirms. Tenjin shares installs and purchases with AppLovin, the ad network that showed the ad, so we can measure and
improve our ad campaigns. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings.

### Ad campaign measurement (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad we rely only on Apple's SKAdNetwork: Apple tells the ad network, and our measurement partner
Tenjin, which campaign led to an install, without any device identifier.

### Game Center, Google Play Games and saved progress

Your high scores and saved progress go to Apple (Game Center and iCloud) or Google (Google Play Games) under your own
account, so your progress survives a reinstall. We do not receive that data; Apple's and Google's privacy policies
cover it.

## How we use it

To run and improve the game, fix problems, balance difficulty and prices, deliver your purchases and, on Android,
measure which ad campaigns bring new players. We do not use it to build an advertising profile of you.

## Who receives it

- Google: Firebase Analytics, Google Play purchases and Google Play Games.
- Apple: App Store purchases, Game Center, iCloud and SKAdNetwork.
- Unity Technologies: purchase processing.
- Tenjin: ad campaign measurement on Android, and SKAdNetwork reports on iPhone and iPad.
- AppLovin: installs and purchases on Android, through Tenjin.

These companies process the data for the purposes above under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose data
when the law requires it.

## How long it is kept

Google Analytics keeps event-level analytics data for no more than 14 months. Tenjin and AppLovin keep campaign data
under their own policies. Data stored by the game on your device stays there until you uninstall the game.

## Your choices

- Uninstalling the game stops all collection.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
- There are no accounts, so we cannot look you up by name. To ask what we hold or to ask us to delete it, email us
  with your platform and roughly when you installed the game, and we will delete whatever we can associate with you.

## Children

Tread Shred is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children
under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

Everything the game sends travels encrypted over HTTPS.

## Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date above.
